# Refund policy — Stillworks

**Version:** draft 1 · **Effective:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Contact:** [SUPPORT EMAIL]

## 1. The policy

**14 days from purchase, no questions asked.** Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** from the address you bought
with, or use the refund flow in **[MERCHANT OF RECORD]**'s customer portal. Your licence is revoked and
the money goes back to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.

We can refund because the product is genuinely local: nothing we hand you is consumed or degraded by
being looked at. We do not add conditions, and we do not require you to explain.

## 2. After 14 days

Refunds are at our discretion, and in practice we say no — except when the Software fundamentally does
not do what the documentation says it does. If something is broken, tell us: a fix, a workaround or (if
it is our fault and it is recent) a refund is usually the outcome anyway.

## 3. What is not refundable

- **Model usage.** You pay your own AI provider directly under their terms. We cannot refund what we
  never received and cannot see. If a provider bills you unexpectedly, that is with them.
- **Update renewals past 14 days**, unless support is impossible to reach.
- **Licences bought in error** where the error is simply "I did not need it" after the window — though
  inside 14 days, that reason is fine.

## 4. Withdrawal rights, if you are in the EU/UK/EEA

Distance-selling rules give consumers 14 days to withdraw. Because a licence delivers digital content
immediately, that right is normally lost once you consent to start and acknowledge the waiver at
checkout. **We do not rely on that.** Our 14-day refund is ours to give, on top of whatever your local
law requires, and we will not argue the point.

## 5. Cancellations and updates

There is nothing to cancel: the licence is perpetual and does not auto-renew. Renewing for another year
of updates is a separate, explicit purchase. If you stop renewing, the Software keeps working.

## 6. Upgrades and downgrades

A Personal licence can be upgraded to a Site licence by paying the difference — email us rather than
buying twice.

## 7. Abuse

Serial buying and refunding to use the Software for free will get the licence key revoked. This has never
come up. It is here so the rule is written down before it is needed.
